# Meeting Talking Points

## What to Say (Simple Business Language)
- "We’re using a documentation-first demo to ensure clarity and transparency."
- "All demo steps are visible and reproducible from the documentation."
- "This approach allows you to see exactly what the solution does, without hidden steps."
- "We can adapt the process to your feedback in real time."

## What Not to Claim
- Do not claim the system is fully automated end-to-end.
- Do not claim all edge cases are handled.
- Do not promise production readiness unless confirmed.

## How to Explain Docs-First Demo Choice Confidently
- "A docs-first demo ensures everyone is on the same page and can follow each step."
- "It’s the best way to validate our process and outcomes before moving to a live system."
- "This method builds trust and invites your input at every stage."
